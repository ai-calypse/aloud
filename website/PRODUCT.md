# Product

## Register

brand

## Users
People on a Mac who type all day (founders, engineers, writers, operators) and are tired of switching windows to get small things done. They arrive from a link or a search, decide in a few seconds whether Aloud is for them, and want to download it or see exactly what it does before trusting it with their keyboard and their apps.

## Product Purpose
Aloud is voice dictation and voice commands for the Mac. Hold a key, speak, and clean text lands in whatever app has focus; or say what you want done (reminders, messages, files, music, connected apps) and Aloud does it, showing each action before it happens. The website exists to get the visitor to download it, by making the idea land immediately and proving the claims with real commands. Success is a download from someone who already understands what the key does.

## Brand Personality
Playful, warm, nostalgic. The voice is plain, short and confident; active verbs, no hype words, real commands instead of adjectives. The tin-can phone is the core metaphor: speaking into something simple and having it arrive. Pixel art gives the brand a hand-made, remembered-from-childhood feel while the copy stays grown-up and exact.

## Anti-references
- Generic SaaS: purple/blue gradient washes, identical icon-and-heading card grids, stock-feeling sections.
- Dark neon AI tool: near-black page, glowing accent, sparkle icons.
- Corporate enterprise: stiff, jargon-heavy, stock photography.
- Childish or cutesy: cartoonish and unserious. Aloud can send messages and touch files for the user, so the playfulness must never undercut trust.

## Design Principles
1. **Show the metaphor, then the proof.** The pixel tin-can scene carries the idea in one glance; every section after it earns trust with a real command or a real behaviour, not an adjective.
2. **Playful surface, serious substance.** Warmth lives in imagery and type character. Copy, spacing and controls stay precise so the product reads as dependable.
3. **One memorable thing per screen.** The animated scene is the hero's moment; everything around it stays quiet and disciplined.
4. **Say what happens.** Buttons and labels name the exact action, in plain verbs and sentence case.
5. **Pixel crispness is a rule.** Pixel art is never smoothed, blurred or scaled to fractions; it is displayed sharp and at integer-feeling sizes.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
WCAG 2.2 AA as the floor (assumed; not yet confirmed with the owner). Animated imagery must respect `prefers-reduced-motion` with a still frame, text over imagery must meet 4.5:1 (3:1 for large text), and every control needs visible keyboard focus and a touch target of at least 44px.
